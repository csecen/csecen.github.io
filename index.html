About Me


Welcome to my webpage. Here you will find some information about me including my interests both in and out of my career, as well as my education and my work experience over the years.


I am currently a machine learning engineer with Leidos, still early in my career. I work in the signal processing field tackling difficult problems for our customers. I also have experience as a software engineer with Leidos and have had a few internships with a wide range of repsonsibilites from testing, to helping write scripts and tools, to web development. I have learned a great deal from all my experiences and hope to continue to learn more as I further my career.

Outside of work, I also enjoy using what I've learned to aid in personal projects. I have several ongoing personal projects that can be found on my github. One is a repository of functions used to visualize different mathematical concepts, mainly for visualization/display purposes. I am also currently working on a few projects geared towards surfing. One is a computer vision project using satellite imagery to locate areas around coastlines that could be potential surf spots. In a similar vein, I'm working on another surf spot locator using bathymetry data and machine learning rather than satellite imagery. Another project of note is a surf forecasting tool, using machine learning and traditional forecasting methods.

Aside from programming I enjoy staying active and getting outside. I like to surf, ski, bike, paddle board, and play touch rugby and volleyball whenever I can.



Education


I started my journey into computer science at the University of Maryland where I earned my B.S. in Computer Science. After working for a couple years in the field as a software engineer I decided to continue my education and pursue a master's degree. In 2024 I earned my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from Drexel University.

Throughout my undergrad years I took a range of courses including Data Structures, Intro to Data Science, Machine Learning, Networks and Security, Algorithms, and Database Design. This variety in course material allowed me to gain a large set of skills while also allowing me to find an area in the field that really interested me, machine learning. My graduate education allowed me to hone my skills in machine learning, taking courses such as Deep Learning, Applied Artificial Intelligence, Quantitative Data Science, Acquisition and Pre-Processing, and Algorithmic Game Theory

My education has helped me greatly to succeed in my career and while I don't anticipate any further higher-level education, I still love and plan to continue my education whether that be through personal projects or online courses.
